About me

I’m a third-semester Computer Science student at the University of Zurich with a strong interest in building software that is efficient, intuitive and well thought out.

I enjoy turning ideas into practical solutions — from simplifying repetitive tasks and improving workflows to building web applications that are both technically solid and pleasant to use.

I naturally pay attention to the details that shape an experience: structure, clarity, interaction and how everything comes together visually. For me, good software should not only work well, but also feel effortless to use.

Currently, I’m focused on strengthening my software and web development skills, building projects and creating products that save time, reduce friction and feel polished from start to finish.
